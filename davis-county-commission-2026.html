Seat B · Contested

Susan Lee vs. Bret Millburn

Republican nominee vs. unaffiliated former commissioner · no Democratic candidate

Susan Lee won a tight Republican primary against two-term incumbent Lorene Kamalu, 50.89% to 49.11% — a 786-vote margin. She now faces Bret Millburn, a three-term former commissioner (2007–2018) who filed as an unaffiliated candidate before the primary was decided. Millburn held Seat A during his tenure, not Seat B — he and Lee have not previously run against each other.

Susan Lee
Republican Nominee

Stated Priorities

  • "Fighting for Taxpayers. Strengthening Our Communities. Protecting Conservative Principles."
  • Five pillars: taxpayer protection, smart growth, public safety, transparency, education partnerships — stated as broad principles, not detailed proposals.
  • Background: accounting degree, bookkeeping/payroll experience, Kaysville City Council, Kaysville Power Commission.
  • Party record: 6 years GOP precinct chair, 3 years Lincoln Club president, 8 years county delegate, 6 years state delegate, 1 year on the Utah GOP State Central Committee.

Direct to Reporters

  • Wants to consolidate Davis County's two dispatch centers (Bountiful and Layton) into one, citing cost savings.
  • Calls Public Infrastructure Districts a "hidden tax."
  • Wants budget cuts to start with a review of county personnel.
  • Has said she'd like to see prayer reinstated at Davis County Commission meetings — a position that doesn't appear on her official platform page.

Finance — all 4 disclosure reports, reconciled

  • $29,253.29 raised, $26,921.22 spent, $2,332.07 on hand
  • Self-funded $16,298.40 — 55.7% of everything raised
  • Institutional donors: Utah Credit Union PAC $3,000; Davis County Republican Party $4,285

Endorsements

  • John Crofts, sitting third Davis County commissioner (not up this cycle)
  • Karianne Lisonbee, outgoing state representative and 2026 congressional candidate
  • Utah Republican Veterans' Caucus (pre-primary, May 2026)
Note: Lee is the mother of outgoing Rep. Trevor Lee (HD-16), who lost his own 2026 primary and will not appear on the November ballot. Her platform does not reference his legislative record.
Bret Millburn
Unaffiliated

Stated Priorities

  • "People over politics. Experience over partisanship. Results over rhetoric." Explicitly frames the race as non-partisan.
  • Fiscal responsibility; a countywide economic development plan built with the county's 15 cities; public safety framed around addiction and growth; named transportation projects (I-15 rebuild, FrontRunner double-tracking, the Davis–Salt Lake Community Connector); better county-city communication.
  • Served three terms (2007–2018) on the commission, in Seat A.

Direct to Reporters

"When I was in office, we actually had a plan... we very clearly spelled out the steps we had taken to avert the tax increase for as long as we could."

Attributes the county's 2025 property tax increase substantially to COVID-era federal money used for ongoing costs rather than one-time expenses. Says he's "fed up with the parties... fed up with the extremes," while describing himself as conservative, not extreme.

Finance

No disclosure reports filed yet. Davis County's Clerk's Office confirmed his first 2026 report is not yet due under the filing calendar — normal timing, not a compliance gap.

Endorsements

Formally endorses Kendalyn Harris for Seat A. Named supporters include several city mayors and councilmembers across Bountiful, West Bountiful, Layton, North Salt Lake, and Farmington.

Seat A · Unopposed in the General

Kendalyn Harris

Republican nominee, won a 3-way primary · no opponent in November

Seat A was open because outgoing Commissioner Bob Stevenson vacated it to run for — and win — House District 16. Harris, a former Bountiful mayor, won a competitive three-way Republican primary (42.77%) and faces no opponent in the general.

Kendalyn Harris
Republican Nominee, Unopposed

Stated Priorities

  • Five pillars: open/responsive government, fiscal stewardship, public safety and infrastructure, "smart growth" guided by local voices, local economic development.
  • 12 years in Bountiful government — 8 as councilwoman, 4 as mayor.

Direct to Reporters

  • Questions the cost of a proposed $20M animal control facility.
  • Calls the county jail's roughly $1 million/year in overtime spending "unnecessary."
  • Wants to bring a Bountiful-style "strategy session" priority-setting process to the county — "if everything is important, then nothing is."
  • On the 2025 tax increase: frames the problem as much about inconsistent commissioner messaging as the increase itself, favoring smaller, incremental increases over large surprise ones.

Finance — all 4 disclosure reports, reconciled

  • $69,482.24 raised, $68,963.35 spent — more than double Susan Lee's total, despite running unopposed in the general
  • Self-funded only $500 — 0.7% of her total

Endorsements

An unusually broad institutional sweep: four former Davis County commissioners (including Bret Millburn), nearly every sitting mayor across the county's 15 cities, both area state senators, several state representatives, and the current and a former county sheriff.
