Five Things the Records Show

  1. Two reports list the same help at different amounts. CMV PAC reported spending $5,625 on "Signature Gathering" for her on Jan. 19. Her report first listed $5,625 in-kind from CMV PAC. On April 12 her campaign deleted that entry and reported $1,687.50 instead. Neither report says why.
  2. CMV PAC paid for both candidates in the primary. Its report lists the same $5,625 for Sam Barlow on the same day.
  3. The Doers Network paid her $3,750. Her report lists it as "Utah Doers," Feb. 18. The Doers Network's own roster lists her as the House District 27 candidate. Her filings, and the state, say District 17.
  4. The Speaker's two committees gave $1,700. House Speaker Leadership PAC gave $700 and the Utah House Republican Election Committee gave $1,000.
  5. Her disclosure form answers "NA" on every required line. It names her husband and describes him as "a doctor in Plastic Surgery." His practice is Bitner Facial Plastic Surgery of Utah in Layton. The form asks for a spouse's employer only if the candidate believes it may be a conflict.
