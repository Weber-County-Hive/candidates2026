Grades on the five questions

Every item is graded on the same five questions used across this project: Power, Transparency, Financial accountability, Environmental impact and Community impact. A C means the record meets what Utah law requires, the status quo. A B or A means the candidate went beyond it, and the record shows it. D means something is vague or bundled, or a public check is weakened; F means something required is missing, or a check is removed with nothing in its place. On reports, one required item missing is graded D, since it can be a mistake; several missing from the same report is graded F. On disclosure forms, an item voters can't see anywhere else (a company, a board seat, a job) left off is graded D; two left off, D−; three or more, F, a pattern. Legislative pay, which is public, left off with nothing else missing is graded D+. Anything named somewhere on the form counts as listed, and a form not on file is graded F. (Disclosure-form ladder added Oct. 3, 2026.) How a grade can change: candidates can file an amended report or an updated disclosure form at any time. When the missing or unclear item is fixed on the public record, we update the grade and note the date. Feedback appreciated: candidates and readers can send corrections or records we missed to webercountyhive@gmail.com, and we check every one against the public record.

Two timing rules apply to every candidate on this site: a report filed by its due date is on time, and each contribution should appear within 31 days of the date it was received.

Overall is the average of the graded questions (A = 4, B = 3, C = 2, D = 1, F = 0; N/A and dashes are left out). A plus or minus shows where the average falls between letters, and an average exactly halfway counts toward the higher letter with a minus. Added Oct. 2, 2026.

Past relationships: the Gibson family network

Wilcox's own family history connects him directly to a PAC that has funded his campaigns, established through cross-referenced public obituaries. Wayne B. Gibson (d. December 2021) — longtime Weber County Planning Commission member, nearly 30-year Weber Basin Water District President, Weber County Farm Bureau President, and a Reagan-era appointee to the State ASC Committee — had three children: Kerry Gibson, Ron Gibson, and Melanie Gibson Wilcox. Melanie married Roy Wilcox; their son is Ryan Wilcox. Kerry Gibson married Katrina Gibson.

That makes Kerry Gibson Ryan Wilcox's uncle, and Katrina Gibson his aunt by marriage. Neither relationship appears on any campaign filing by either party; Utah's campaign finance reports and disclosure forms do not ask candidates to list relatives.

Kerry Gibson's career in Utah politics

The uncle at the center of this network is not a private citizen who happened to fund a PAC. He is one of Weber County's best-known former officials, with a career spanning elected office, a governor's cabinet, and a failed congressional bid:

The documented flow of money

Kerry Gibson is a registered Utah lobbyist (since 1/17/2021, via NRAE Solutions LLC), with clients including Ogden City, Plain City, Mountain Green Sewer Improvement District, and Wolf Creek Management. Katrina Gibson founded Utah Forward PAC and is listed as its CFO and registered agent, funded by developers including Nilson Homes, Building Construction Partners, Lewis, and Dunleavy.

In January 2026, Utah Forward PAC paid Ryan Wilcox $3,000. That same month, Wilcox personally donated $500 to Katrina Gibson's own Weber County Commission campaign (jointly with his wife, Kristina). Wilcox then publicly endorsed Katrina Gibson for Commission Seat A.

Separately, Utah Forward PAC also paid Steven Gibson — a cousin of Kerry Gibson's, confirmed via a public Facebook exchange between the two — $1,000 on 7/29/2025, listed as "Donations." Wilcox's own campaign additionally paid someone named Ryder Wilcox $1,750 (1/27/2026, "Campaign Expense") — a possible family member whose relationship to the candidate has not been confirmed.

What isn't disclosed
Katrina Gibson's own candidate filing does not disclose Kerry Gibson's lobbying relationships with Ogden City and Plain City — municipalities she would have voted on as a Weber County Commissioner. Neither Wilcox's nor Gibson's campaign filings disclose that PAC money and personal donations moving between them involve family members.
