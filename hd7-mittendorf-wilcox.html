An unresolved donor-identification mismatch, not a resolved one
Wilcox's report lists a 6/9/2026 expenditure as "Richard Hyer Campaign – Donations – $500," implying a personal gift from Wilcox to fellow candidate Richard Hyer's county commission campaign. But Hyer's own 2026 report lists that same-date $500 gift as coming from an entity called "Friends of Ryan Wilcox" — not from Wilcox personally. Whether this is a personal gift misreported as an entity, an unregistered PAC operating without disclosure, or a bookkeeping error on one side, remains unresolved.

The PAC is the distribution arm of Lincoln Hill Partners, a lobbying firm co-founded by Lincoln Shurtz and Casey Hill. It's funded upstream through Fides LLC, a money-collection vehicle that has moved more than $332,500 through the network since 2023. What is documented: the distribution side — a long list of Utah legislators who've received Lincoln Hill Partners PAC money, and Lincoln Shurtz's dual role as Fides LLC's manager and, separately, Director of Government Affairs for the Utah Association of Counties. What is NOT yet documented, and shouldn't be asserted as fact: the specific individual or corporate names who gave money directly into Fides LLC or Lincoln Hill PAC itself. That upstream donor list has not been itemized by name in the research behind this piece.

Past relationships: the Gibson family network

Wilcox's own family history connects him directly to a PAC that has funded his campaigns, established through cross-referenced public obituaries. Wayne B. Gibson (d. December 2021) — longtime Weber County Planning Commission member, nearly 30-year Weber Basin Water District President, Weber County Farm Bureau President, and a Reagan-era appointee to the State ASC Committee — had three children: Kerry Gibson, Ron Gibson, and Melanie Gibson Wilcox. Melanie married Roy Wilcox; their son is Ryan Wilcox. Kerry Gibson married Katrina Gibson.

That makes Kerry Gibson Ryan Wilcox's uncle, and Katrina Gibson his aunt by marriage. Neither relationship has been disclosed on any campaign filing by either party.

Kerry Gibson's career in Utah politics

The uncle at the center of this network is not a private citizen who happened to fund a PAC. He is one of Weber County's best-known former officials, with a career spanning elected office, a governor's cabinet, and a failed congressional bid:

His county commission tenure was under criminal investigation
Gibson resigned his Weber County Commission seat amid an Ogden Police Department investigation, opened in late 2017, into allegations he misused county equipment, personnel, and funds — including diverting resources from a federally funded river project on his family's dairy farm, an employee's claim she was directed to collect political donations while on the job, and a PAC event held at the Ogden airport. The investigation concluded in May 2018 with no charges filed. His later tenure at the Utah Department of Agriculture and Food was separately the subject of a state audit citing staff turnover, rising travel spending, procedural errors, and conflict-of-interest concerns.

The documented flow of money

Kerry Gibson is a registered Utah lobbyist (since 1/17/2021, via NRAE Solutions LLC), with clients including Ogden City, Plain City, Mountain Green Sewer Improvement District, and Wolf Creek Management. Katrina Gibson founded and controls Utah Forward PAC, funded by developers including Nilson Homes, Building Construction Partners, Lewis, and Dunleavy.

In January 2026, Utah Forward PAC paid Ryan Wilcox $3,000. That same month, Wilcox personally donated $500 to Katrina Gibson's own Weber County Commission campaign (jointly with his wife, Kristina). Wilcox then publicly endorsed Katrina Gibson for Commission Seat A.

Separately, Utah Forward PAC also paid Steven Gibson — a cousin of Kerry Gibson's, confirmed via a public Facebook exchange between the two — $1,000 on 7/29/2025, listed as "Donations." Wilcox's own campaign additionally paid someone named Ryder Wilcox $1,750 (1/27/2026, "Campaign Expense") — a possible family member whose relationship to the candidate has not been confirmed.

What isn't disclosed
Katrina Gibson's own candidate filing does not disclose Kerry Gibson's lobbying relationships with Ogden City and Plain City — municipalities she would have voted on as a Weber County Commissioner. Neither Wilcox's nor Gibson's campaign filings disclose that PAC money and personal donations moving between them involve family members.
