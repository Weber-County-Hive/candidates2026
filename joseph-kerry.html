What Kerry's disclosures leave out

Kerry's published 2026 conflict-of-interest statement identifies Ensign College as his employer. It also lists Littlecloud Media and Bible Rescue among his private entities, and identifies Colonial Flag Foundation and Bible Rescue among his organizational positions.

But it does not include Path Forward Utah.

That omission is confirmed through two independent public-record systems. Utah's corporate record identifies Kerry as a director of Path Forward Utah, first formed as an LLC on January 11, 2022 and converted to a nonprofit corporation on August 1, 2023. The nonprofit's IRS returns identify him as vice president in 2023 and chair in both 2024 and 2025.

Statutory requirement
Utah Code §20A-11-1604 expressly requires State Board of Education members to disclose paid or unpaid positions on boards of directors, as well as other covered leadership positions. Kerry's Path Forward Utah directorship does not appear on any of the four USBE disclosures reviewed by Weber County Hive.

His disclosure also omits Joe for Utah, LLC, organized March 7, 2022, which identifies Kerry as manager. The registry entry establishes his management role but does not, by itself, establish whether he owns the company or receives compensation from it. It also omits Colonial Specialty Co., Inc., a Utah entity formed in 1989 where Kerry is listed as registered agent.

The same form contains another unexplained inconsistency: Kerry entered "N/A" for his spouse's name while identifying Ballet West as the spouse's current and previous employer. In the following section, he supplied names and employment information for two unrelated adults residing in his household — Jensen Chertudi (Brighton Ski Resort) and Txanton Chertudi (Lowe's Home Goods).

What the records don't establish: what Kerry reported, what the corporate and IRS records report, and where those records do not match — that's what's documented here. Why the information was omitted is not established by these records.

Path Forward Utah

Kerry chairs the board of Path Forward Utah, a 501(c)(3) whose stated purpose is "empowering and uplifting minority voices while fostering positive change in society." Its 2025 Form 990-EZ, the most recent available, shows an organization that grew quickly and pays one officer a substantial salary.

What this does and doesn't show: Renda's $60,000 compensation is approximately 36% of the organization's annual revenue and 47% of its total expenses. The abbreviated public return does not disclose who approved that compensation, what comparison data was used, or whether Renda participated in the decision. Those are open governance questions — not evidence the compensation was unlawful. Filing the abbreviated Form 990-EZ is not itself unusual; organizations under $200,000 in annual receipts and $500,000 in assets generally qualify to use it, and Path Forward Utah was under both thresholds.

What the nonprofit says it does

The 990-EZ's own program-service description states Path Forward Utah organized "two multi-day education policy retreats bringing together State School Board members, legislators, community organizations and the Utah State Auditor's Office" on school discipline, UPPAC reform, and education priorities — plus separate events "bringing together elected officials, faith leaders, immigrant communities, legislators, State School Board" on immigration, housing, and inflation. A third program, "Civics Begins With Me," reports reaching 380 students through the National Civics Bee and teaching civics lessons to 70 students.

A 501(c)(3) may conduct nonpartisan civic education, public forums, issue advocacy, and limited lobbying. It may not support or oppose a candidate, contribute to a campaign, or give one candidate organizational resources without equivalent opportunity for opponents. The IRS examines the full facts and circumstances of each case; these records alone do not establish a violation.

The Children First Education Fund contract

Kim Coleman — founder of the Children First Education Fund and a documented Kerry campaign donor — has a role that goes well beyond simply founding a scholarship-granting organization.

Contract USBE210055CT — Scholarship Granting Organization Services

USBE selected Children First Education Fund through a competitive procurement to administer Utah's Special Needs Opportunity Scholarship Program. CFEF's responsibilities under the contract: fundraising, operating the application system, determining eligibility, making award decisions on an income-based sliding scale, issuing state tax-credit certificates, tracking donations against the statutory cap, monitoring continuing eligibility, and maintaining student records. USBE's role: creating the tax-credit certificate and monitoring CFEF's compliance.

Coleman's named role in the contract: point of contact, CEO and board chair, "essential key personnel," and CFEF's "Program & Contract Manager."

Financial structure: CFEF permitted to retain up to 5% of donations for administration plus 3% for marketing/fundraising, capped at a maximum of $2,901,144 across the contract period — a ceiling, not proof of what was actually paid or retained.

The agreement took effect March 11, 2021 and listed a termination date of December 31, 2025 unless extended. USBE continues to identify Children First as the program's SGO on its website, but the public contract reviewed here does not establish whether the agreement was extended or replaced after 2025.

The contract predates Kerry's 2022 election to the board, so these records do not establish that Kerry participated in selecting CFEF, or that Coleman currently receives compensation. What they establish is an ongoing organizational relationship between Kerry's board, his campaign donor, and the policy mechanism — scholarship granting organizations — now being promoted publicly.

Colonial Specialty Co. and his largest donor

Kerry also serves as registered agent for Colonial Specialty Co., Inc., a Utah corporation formed in 1989, operating from the same Washington Boulevard address as Littlecloud Media and Bible Rescue. That role does not appear anywhere on his Conflict of Interest disclosure.

What the company does, and who it represents

Colonial Specialty Co. does business as Colonial Flag, a Sandy, Utah retailer founded in 1979 that sells and services flagpoles, custom flags, banners, and memorial and cemetery flag equipment, along with gift items and home decor.

Its officers, per the Utah Division of Corporations: Paul Swenson (President, Owner/CEO), David K. Swenson (Director), and Elizabeth R. Swenson (Director, Secretary) — all at the company's Sandy address.

Who Paul Swenson is to this race
Paul Swenson is Kerry's largest documented campaign donor — $3,000 total ($1,000 cash plus $2,000 in-kind), both later deleted from Kerry's campaign finance filings with no explanation given. Kerry serves as registered agent for the company Swenson owns and runs.

Colonial Specialty Co. is a separate legal entity from Colonial Flag Foundation, the nonprofit where Kerry serves as Trustee — the two share branding but are not the same organization; avoid conflating them.

The governing overlap

Path Forward Utah's board connects to Kerry's donor network and to state party politics in ways that go beyond a single contract.

  • Jamie Renda founded Path Forward Utah and serves as its registered agent. She personally contributed $10.76 to Kerry's campaign on April 17, 2026.
  • Jaime Wadman, the third director, is an active member of the Utah Republican State Central Committee, and a confirmed co-sponsor of the SCC's "Resolution in Support of Repealing R277-328 and All CRT Praxis in Utah Schools" — R277-328 is a USBE administrative rule, the exact board Kerry sits on.
  • Kim Coleman — CFEF's named contract manager and a Kerry donor — is also a Utah Republican State Central Committee member, confirmed as a co-sponsor alongside Wadman on a separate SCC resolution.

Together, the records show a three-sided network: Wadman and Coleman serve together on the State Central Committee; Wadman and Kerry serve together as Path Forward Utah directors; and Coleman is a donor to Kerry's campaign.

"Education Opportunity Night," August 26, 2026

Kerry describes himself as the host, in his own announcement, of an event featuring Congressman Burgess Owens at The Copper Nickel in Ogden. The event is presented by Path Forward Utah and "Path4Utah," with Americans for Prosperity providing refreshments. Its stated topics — Scholarship Granting Organizations, Workforce Pell Grants, and Learning and Employment Records — track directly with Owens's active congressional agenda as chair of the House Higher Education and Workforce Development Subcommittee.

The State Board of Education does not approve Workforce Pell programs; federal law assigns certification to the governor and final approval to the U.S. Secretary of Education. But Kerry's role as host places a sitting state education policymaker at the center of a federal policy push that directly touches his own donor network.
