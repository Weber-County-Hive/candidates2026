Five Things the Records Show

IN PLAIN WORDSNobody in this race has been a state senator. One is a city council member with a large campaign, one is a first-time Forward Party candidate, and one has raised very little.

  1. Catten raised $85,846 in cash and spent $52,049.88 by June 11, before a contested Democratic primary. Elevate Strategies received $35,685.68 of that (68.6%). Individuals gave about 71% of her money.
  2. A PAC named Elevate PAC gave Catten $5,000 on June 11. It shares a name with her main vendor. The filings don’t show whether the two are related.
  3. Blue Star Steel, the company her disclosure form lists as her spouse’s employer, gave her campaign $2,000. She and her spouse gave $6,000 more.
  4. Smith raised $23,018 in cash, 52% from outside Utah (by ZIP code), including $2,604.48 from California and $2,500 from Arkansas. The Forward Party gave $300.
  5. Mahoney raised $768.85 in cash and gave $418.52 of in-kind help himself. His export runs through Sept. 24, the most current filing of the three.
