What this diagram is actually saying

Confirmed: Bob Stevenson vacated his own Davis County Commission Seat A specifically to run for HD16 — one person, one deliberate move up, not two unrelated vacancies.
Confirmed: Susan Lee, who just won her own primary for Commission Seat B, is Trevor Lee's mother — the incumbent Bob Stevenson beat. Not a donor or financial connection, just worth knowing when her name and her son's name both come up in the same cycle.
Unconfirmed: Whether Jerry Stevenson (retiring SD-6) is related to Bob Stevenson (incoming HD16) remains an open question — the only hard data point is the $1,000 gift filed under a shared mailing address. No obituary, property record, or public bio found on either side confirms or rules it out.
Confirmed: Jerry Stevenson personally funded both handoffs in this cycle — $1,000 to Bob Stevenson (HD16) on 6/11/26, and $500 to his own successor Tamara Tran (SD-6) on 6/22/26.
A third Stevenson, unresolved: Jed D. Stevenson gave $4,000 to Tamara Tran the same day as Jerry Stevenson's $500 gift (6/22/26). His mailing address — 706 E 1000 S, Kaysville — doesn't match Jerry's Layton address, so this is a separate open lead, not confirmation of anything already flagged. Worth checking whether Jed is related to either Jerry or Bob before this cycle closes out.
